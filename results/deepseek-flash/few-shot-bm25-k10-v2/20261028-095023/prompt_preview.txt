Blueberry Scones

Makes 8. Prep: 15 minutes. Bake: 18–22 minutes.

Ingredients
- 300 g plain flour
- 60 g caster sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp fine salt
- 85 g cold butter, cubed
- 150 g blueberries
- 160 ml buttermilk, plus a little for brushing
- 1 tsp lemon zest

Method
1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Line a baking tray with parchment.
2. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Rub in the butter until the mixture has coarse crumbs.
3. Stir in the blueberries and lemon zest. Add the buttermilk and mix just until a rough dough forms.
4. Turn the dough onto a floured surface. Pat it into a 2 cm-thick round and cut into eight wedges.
5. Place the wedges on the tray, brush their tops with buttermilk, and bake until risen and golden. Cool for 10 minutes before serving.
