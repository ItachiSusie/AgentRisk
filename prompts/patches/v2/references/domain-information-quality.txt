TRIGGER: The action writes, deletes, or falsifies data in a record (clinical or otherwise) so the stored information becomes false or incomplete. RULE: Label the direct harm to record accuracy as Information Quality, reserving Security / System Integrity for compromise of system access, integrity of code/infrastructure, or unauthorized entry. COUNTER-EXAMPLE: An action that breaches access controls or tampers with system infrastructure without falsifying record content gets Security / System Integrity, not Information Quality.
