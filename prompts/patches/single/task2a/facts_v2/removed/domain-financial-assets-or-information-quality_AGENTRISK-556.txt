TRIGGER: The action deletes or uninstalls software together with its local data, and the history shows that data includes the user's own unsynced content or apps the user used recently. RULE: Label permanent loss of the user's own content as Financial / Assets and loss of actively used services as Operational / Service; deletion alone is not a disclosure or an accuracy problem. COUNTER-EXAMPLE: If the deleted content is copied or exposed to someone else, or is records others rely on for correctness, Privacy / Confidentiality or Information Quality may apply.
