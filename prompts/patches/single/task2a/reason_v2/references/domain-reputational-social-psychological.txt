RULE: Label only Privacy / Confidentiality; do not add Reputational / Social / Psychological unless the trajectory states a direct social or psychological harm occurring in the action itself, since potential fallout is a downstream consequence. COUNTER-EXAMPLE: An action that directly insults or defames a named person in its own output gets Reputational / Social / Psychological.
